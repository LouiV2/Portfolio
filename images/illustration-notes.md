# Portfolio concept illustrations

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. These images illustrate skill categories
and communication; they do not depict Louis, his workspace, or completed projects.
The generated originals were converted to JPEG for website delivery.

## Assets and subjects

- `skills-programming.jpg`: a sculptural laptop with abstract code-like marks and a luminous branching circuit rising from its keyboard, representing programming
- `skills-creative.jpg`: a digital drawing tablet with a stylus, a floating wireframe geometric sculpture and golden design curves, representing creative design and interactive development
- `skills-productivity.jpg`: an organized workspace with three floating translucent document panels, a calendar grid without numbers and a notebook, representing everyday productivity
- `contact-connection.jpg`: a sculptural luminous envelope at the center of concentric communication rings with fine connection paths, representing starting a conversation

## Final prompt template

Each subject above replaced `{subject}` in this prompt, with transparency disabled:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: personal portfolio website illustration. Create one polished cinematic 3D editorial illustration of {subject}. Tony Stark workshop / Batcave computer inspired visual language: angular graphite metal, finely etched HUD rings, warm signal yellow light, slate blue background #151d28, steel gray and off-white details. Square composition, central subject fully visible with generous margins, clear silhouette, sophisticated restrained lighting bright enough to read on dark website. No people, no logos, no readable text, no numbers, no watermark, no fake metrics. This is a conceptual illustration, not a screenshot.
